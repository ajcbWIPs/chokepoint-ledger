Soil values are SoilGrids 250 m model estimates for 0 to 5 cm. Zones are approximate centres and radii for orientation, not survey boundaries. Salinity is not in SoilGrids, so it comes from the zone preset or your own soil test. Crop limits use FAO salt tolerance data and approximate climate ranges. Check any recommendation against a local soil test and agronomist.
